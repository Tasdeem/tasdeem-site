THE PROBLEM

The data exists. The architecture behind it usually doesn't.

Most organisations have dashboards, emission figures and supplier questionnaires. What they rarely have is the operational evidence that makes those numbers defensible when a verifier, a procurement team or a regulator looks underneath them.

  • ✕ GHG figures built on assumptions, with no trail back to source data.
  • ✕ Materiality assessments that can't show how topics were chosen.
  • ✕ Policies that exist on paper, with no owner, workflow or board oversight.
  • ✕ KPIs with no baseline, so progress can't be demonstrated.
  • ✕ Impact practices not yet mapped to the requirements of the new B Lab Standards.

"A UAE contractor shared their sustainability strategy for a preliminary review. Within 20 minutes we found no board engagement, no materiality process, no data collection system, and KPIs with no baseline. They thought they were ahead of the market. They were starting from zero."
